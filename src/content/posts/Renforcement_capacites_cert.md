## About the Certification
 
Completed a capacity-building training on **club creation and the management of sociocultural events**, delivered by the Bureau International d'Ingénierie et de Conseil (BIIC) at the Institut Préparatoire aux Études d'Ingénieurs de Kairouan (IPEIK), as part of the PAQ-DGSU "Initiative vers l'Excellence" program supporting student employability.
 
The training covered practical fundamentals of founding and running a student club, and organizing and managing cultural and social events from planning to execution.
